Connect Prembly to your registration flow.

  1. Download the ZIP. Open Plugins > Add New Plugin > Upload Plugin, upload, install, and activate HDYHaus Identity Verification.
  2. Enable Settings > General > Anyone can register.
  3. In Prembly, choose Sandbox or Production before creating an SDK Setup widget. Sandbox is recommended for testing; you can start with Production if you are ready for live checks. Create a separate widget for each environment you use.
  4. On Prembly's Brand Details screen, enter your site name, a short visitor-facing description, and your brand colour. Set Redirect URL to your site's WordPress registration URL, normally https://your-domain.com/wp-login.php?action=register. Replace the example domain with your own and retain any WordPress subdirectory or local development port.
  5. Leave Prembly's Webhook URL blank. The plugin confirms verification sessions directly and has no Prembly webhook receiver. Configure your document and face checks, then save the widget.
  6. Use Copy Config ID for the saved widget's Configuration ID. Open its row menu and choose Integration for the Widget Key, beginning wdgt_. Open Identity Verification > Settings > Prembly in WordPress and enter both values in the matching Sandbox or Live fields. Choose the matching active environment, enable Prembly, and save.
  7. Open Identity Verification > Registration to customize consent wording, provider policy URLs, and your website terms or disclaimer link. The optional Success Redirect URL is the same-site page shown after WordPress creates the account. Use your registration URL for Prembly's Redirect URL.
  8. Add identity verification information to your site's privacy policy. Suggested text is available under Settings > Privacy.
  9. Test successful verification, blocked unverified registration, and password-setup emails before opening registration to visitors.
