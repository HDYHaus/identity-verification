WordPress registration

Why it exists

A verified identity should come before a user account.

An email address alone does not establish who is registering. HDYHaus Identity Verification adds identity verification before the account exists, so you can require a confirmed identity from the start.

  • Block account creation when verification is missing or cannot be confirmed.
  • Check the provider result on your server before allowing registration.
  • Keep the native WordPress form and password-setup email flow.

What it does

Identity verification, built into registration.

A focused gate for native WordPress account creation.

01

Check the status, session ID, widget configuration, and registrant email before creating an account.

02

Who it is for

For sites that need to know who is joining.

For single-site WordPress communities and service portals where new accounts need a confirmed identity. Developers can add it to an existing native registration flow.

  • Works with wp-login.php?action=register.
  • WooCommerce, BuddyPress, MemberPress, Ultimate Member, and other custom registration forms are not currently supported.
  • Multisite and network activation are not currently supported.

How it works

Verification comes before account creation.

1

Register

The person enters their username, name, and email on the native WordPress registration screen.

2

Consent

They accept the displayed consent before opening the verification provider.

3

Verify

Prembly performs the identity checks selected by the site owner.

4

Confirm

WordPress confirms the session on the server, creates the account, and sends the password-setup email.

Getting started

Connect Prembly to your registration flow.

  1. Download the ZIP. Open Plugins > Add New Plugin > Upload Plugin, upload, install, and activate HDYHaus Identity Verification.
  2. Enable Settings > General > Anyone can register.
  3. In Prembly, choose Sandbox or Production, then create an SDK Setup widget. Sandbox is recommended for testing; you can start with Production if ready for live checks. Create a separate widget for each environment you use.
  4. On Prembly’s Brand Details screen, enter your site name, a short visitor-facing description, and your brand colour. Set Redirect URL to your site’s WordPress registration URL, normally https://your-domain.com/wp-login.php?action=register. Replace the example domain with your own and retain any WordPress subdirectory or local development port.
  5. Leave Webhook URL blank: this plugin confirms verification sessions directly and has no Prembly webhook receiver. Configure the document and face checks appropriate for your site, then save the widget. Use Copy Config ID for its Configuration ID and the widget’s Integration panel for its Widget Key, beginning wdgt_.
  6. Open Identity Verification > Settings > Prembly in WordPress. Enter the Widget Key and Configuration ID in the matching Sandbox or Live fields.
  7. Choose the matching active environment (Sandbox / Test or Live / Production), select Enable Prembly, and save.
  8. Open Identity Verification > Registration to customize consent wording, provider policy URLs, and your website terms or disclaimer link. The optional Success Redirect URL is a same-site page shown after WordPress creates the account. Prembly’s Redirect URL uses your registration page.
  9. Add identity verification information to the site privacy policy. Suggested text is available under Settings > Privacy.
  10. Test successful verification, blocked unverified registration, and password-setup emails before opening registration to visitors.

Verification data

Documents stay with your verification provider.

Prembly processes identity documents and verification checks. HDYHaus Identity Verification stores normalized verification and consent metadata in WordPress, including a session reference and site-specific one-way fingerprints.

  • Document images, document numbers, selfies, biometric information, and full provider reports are not copied into WordPress.
  • Verification metadata works with WordPress personal-data export and erasure tools.
  • One-way session and identity fingerprints remain after erasure or account deletion to prevent reuse.
